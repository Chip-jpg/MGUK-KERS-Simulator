# v0.7.2 — manual-drive presets

The driving simulator restores 2025 (120 kW) and 2026 (350 kW) buttons using
`kers-braking/model.ts` PRESETS and their existing source links. The selected
preset is marked visually and through `aria-pressed`; manual power edits update
that indication. Selection updates deploy/harvest and battery charge/discharge
limits together, then resets speed, pedals, history and energy counters to the
user's initial SoC. Other vehicle settings and pause state are preserved.

The comparison isolates MGU-K power. It does not simulate MGU-H or change the
illustrative combustion engine, vehicle mass or battery capacity. The expandable
explanation makes this scope explicit. The manufacturer's overview confirms the
120→350 kW increase and MGU-H removal:
[Honda 2026 overview](https://global.honda/en/F1/features/2026_Commentary/regulations/).
