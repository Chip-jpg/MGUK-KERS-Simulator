# Open facts

Real-world numbers and quotations used in `content/theory/**` that do not come from the
teacher's slides. Each one carries an inline "Fonte:" link in the theory text; anything not
fully verified is marked `[DA VERIFICARE]` there and listed here.

Research method: FIA PDFs were fetched directly and text extracted for the
fact check in `/workspace/kers3d/fia-facts.md`. Primary documents:
[TR25](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/documents/fia_2025_formula_1_technical_regulations_-_issue_03_-_2025-04-07.pdf)
= FIA 2025 F1 Technical Regulations, Issue 3 (07 Apr 2025);
[C26](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_c_technical_-_iss_21_-_2026-10-19.pdf)
= FIA 2026 F1 Regulations Section C, Issue 21 (19 Oct 2026).
